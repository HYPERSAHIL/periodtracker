# Research bibliography — women's hormones, menstruation, tracking (102 papers)

Annotated for product decisions in a privacy-first period tracker. ♀ = woman first author.
Companion to the earlier 15-paper brief (Johnson/Setton/Moglia/Worsfold/Bull/STRAW+10/ACOG/Malki/Song/White/Dong/Broad/Levy/Pichon/Nair).

## Cross-cutting product directives

1. Never present calendar ovulation as a single day; demand a marker or show wide bands.
2. Model age-dependent follicular length; ~13d luteal; flag ≤9d luteal, counsel short cycles.
3. Regular bleeding ≠ ovulation (only ~63% ovulatory by progesterone).
4. Uncertainty-first UX; 2-cycle prospective tracking before PMDD-type labels.
5. Perimenopause by STRAW+10 variability staging, not calendar math; 25% of >60d cycles still ovulate.
6. Local-first + granular consent + one-tap delete (Malki/Song/White/Alaattinoglu).
7. Irregular/PCOS/peri/postpartum as first-class modes; teen mode with consent-first design.
8. Validated scales only (0–10 NRS, Endometriosis Symptom Diary items, Rotterdam, FIGO PALM-COEIN).
9. India: Hindi/regional languages, offline-light, low-literacy guides, pre-menarche education.
10. Wearable nocturnal temperature beats spot BBT (wrist sens. 0.62 vs 0.23; Oura error 1.26d vs 3.44d calendar).
